# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
OctoAcme uses a practical, iterative project management lifecycle that keeps teams aligned on customer value, ownership, and measurable outcomes. Work starts by validating the business need, defining success metrics, and aligning stakeholders, then moves into planning with a prioritized backlog, milestones, dependencies, and a clear Definition of Done.

During execution, teams deliver in small increments using project boards, standups, demos, and pull request practices that emphasize testability and review quality. Risks and dependencies are tracked in a risk register and reviewed regularly, while status updates use consistent templates and a single source of truth to keep stakeholders informed and escalations clear.

Release and deployment are standardized through readiness checks, CI/security validation, staged rollouts, and rollback planning. After sprints, milestones, releases, or incidents, retrospectives convert lessons learned into owned action items so teams can continuously improve delivery reliability and collaboration. Roles and personas define accountability across Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, and stakeholders.

## Lifecycle at a Glance
- **Initiation:** Confirm problem, goal, stakeholders, success metrics, and go/no-go.
- **Planning:** Build the backlog, estimate scope, define DoD, map milestones, and capture dependencies.
- **Execution & Tracking:** Deliver incrementally, run team cadences, monitor metrics, and manage blockers.
- **Risk Management & Communication:** Maintain the risk register, publish status updates, and escalate when needed.
- **Release & Deployment:** Complete release readiness checks, deploy safely, verify, and communicate.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement:** Capture what worked, improve what did not, and track follow-up actions.
- **Roles & Personas:** Clarify responsibilities and communication patterns across core contributors.
